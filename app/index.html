⚠ One missable trophy. Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. PowerPyx and GTABase flag it as the only trophy that may be missable: one spot, Lucia’s balcony, is only reachable in Chapter 1, and there is no mission replay. Meditate at every spot the moment you find it, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. GTA Vice City Hub notes no missable is confirmed — this guide flags it anyway, because the cost of missing it is a second playthrough.

About this guide

The list is the 55-trophy PlayStation 5 list as published by PSNProfiles and Exophase and reported in the press on 18–19 November 2026: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden until earned. Every guide is written in our own words for this page; it may draw on other guides, wikis, videos and forum posts, and it names its source beside it — a source is cited, never copied. The official trophy names and descriptions are shown as PlayStation publishes them. Five trophies (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) have no confirmed unlock method yet in any source we found, and their guides say so plainly instead of inventing one.

Progress is stored only in this browser (localStorage). Export downloads a JSON file you can import back on any device. This page loads nothing from the network: every file it uses is part of the guide itself.
